# HexChestHacks
Adds snap points to chests and barrels, allowing you to neatly arrange them side by side, front to back, or vertically.

Customize storage capacity for chests and wardrobes.

Option to remove inventory scrollbars and display all storage slots on a single page.

### Warning
>If you change storage capacity in an existing world, restore all chests to their original vanilla dimensions before disabling or uninstalling this mod. Otherwise, items stored outside the vanilla inventory boundaries may become inaccessible or be lost. Back up your world before making changes.
- In order to shrink chests capacity the chest must be empty.
